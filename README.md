# Online-Job-Recruitment-and-Management-System
The Online Job Recruitment and Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify and improve the recruitment process by connecting job seekers with employers through a centralized online platform

## Actors

1. **Job Seeker:** creates and updates profile, Uploads resumes, searches/filters jobs, submits applications and can see their application statuses.

2. **Employer(Company):** registers their company profile, posts and manages job openings, reviews incoming candidate applications, schedules interviews and updates hiring statuses.

3. **System Administrator:** verifies employer accounts to prevent spam and scams, manages job categories, handles user disputes and monitors overall system analytics.

4. **Guest:** can browse public job boards and view company profiles, but must register to apply or post jobs.
